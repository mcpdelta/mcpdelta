# Security

Delta MCP runs on your computer, and its source code is not public. This repository only presents the app. If you find a security problem in Delta MCP or on [mcpdelta.com](https://mcpdelta.com), we want to hear about it, privately.

## How to report

Write to **[contact@mcpdelta.com](mailto:contact@mcpdelta.com)**.

Please don’t open a public issue, discussion or pull request for a vulnerability.

## What to send

- What you found, and what someone could do with it.
- The steps to reproduce it, or a short proof of concept.
- The Delta MCP version, your operating system and the AI app involved, if any.
- Whether the problem is in the app or on the website.

Please leave out real secrets, passwords and other people’s data.

## What to expect

We read every report and reply as soon as we can. We keep what you send private, and we tell you when the problem is fixed. We don’t promise fixed deadlines.

## While you test

- Use your own computer and your own accounts.
- Don’t read, change or delete data that isn’t yours.
- Give us a reasonable time to fix a problem before you share the details publicly.

## What Delta MCP sends

What leaves your computer, and what never does, is listed in full on the [security page](https://mcpdelta.com/security).
